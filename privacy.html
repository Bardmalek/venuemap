VenueMap Privacy Notice

VenueMap collects only the information needed to operate mapped event booth bookings, vendor applications, sponsor inquiries, receipts, and organizer dashboards.

What We Collect

We may collect business names, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, setup details, payment references, event application notes, and documents submitted for event compliance review.

How We Use It

We use this information to process applications, manage booth inventory, communicate event instructions, support payment and receipt workflows, and help organizers operate their events safely.

Access And Security

Private vendor, sponsor, document, and payment information is restricted through Supabase Row Level Security and server-side functions. Public pages only expose published event information and non-private booth availability.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact VenueMap at hello@venuemap.ca.
