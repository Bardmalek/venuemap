Compliance stance

Built to be boring in the best way.

  • This event is independently organized and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to FIFA, the FIFA World Cup, or any official tournament organizer.
  • FIFA names, marks, logos, slogans, mascots, and protected event branding may not be used by vendors, sponsors, or organizers unless separately authorized.
  • No counterfeit merchandise is permitted. Vendors must sell legally sourced goods only.
  • Food vendors are responsible for required permits, health approvals, insurance, and safe operation.
  • Public match screening, alcohol service, street vending, and sidewalk signage are not part of the launch format unless separately approved.

FAQ

Clear rules, less chaos.

Is entry free?
Yes. Entry is free for attendees. Vendors and sponsors pay for booth or activation space.
Is this an official FIFA event?
No. This is an independently organized community marketplace and is not affiliated with FIFA or any official tournament organizer.
Will matches be shown?
This event is designed as a food, games, and community marketplace. Public match screening is not part of the MVP unless separately licensed and approved.
Can vendors sell jerseys?
Only legally sourced goods are allowed. Counterfeit merchandise and unauthorized use of protected marks, logos, or official branding are not permitted.
Do food vendors need permits?
Food vendors are responsible for required health approvals, insurance, and safe setup. The organizer may request documents before approval.
When do vendors pay?
Applications are reviewed first. Approved vendors receive a payment link or deposit instructions once the site and booth assignment are confirmed.
