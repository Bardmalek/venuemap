FAQ

Clear rules, less chaos.

Is entry free?
Yes. Entry is free for attendees. Vendors and sponsors pay for booth or activation space. There is no attendee ticketing in this event format.
Is this an official FIFA event?
No. This is an independently organized community marketplace and is not affiliated with FIFA or any official tournament organizer. No FIFA names, marks, logos, slogans, or protected branding may be used by vendors or sponsors.
Will matches be shown?
This event is designed as a food, games, and community marketplace. Public match screening is not part of this event format unless separately licensed and approved.
Can vendors sell jerseys or fan merchandise?
Only legally sourced goods are allowed. Counterfeit merchandise and unauthorized use of protected marks, logos, or official branding are not permitted. Vendors selling fan items must be able to demonstrate legal sourcing if requested.
Do food vendors need permits?
Food vendors are responsible for required health approvals, insurance, and safe setup. The organizer may request documents — including health permits, insurance certificates, and propane handling documentation — before approval.
When do vendors pay?
Applications are reviewed first. Approved vendors receive a payment link or deposit instructions once the site and booth assignment are confirmed. No payment is collected at the application stage.
How does the priority deposit work?
A $100 priority deposit moves your application to the front of the review queue. It is fully refunded if the event does not proceed or your application is not accepted. If approved, it is credited toward your booth fee.
